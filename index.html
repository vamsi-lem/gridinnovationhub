2027 Power Generation Reliability Summit

January 28, 2027 · Virtual · 12–5 PM ET
AI and the future of generation reliability

Data center load growth, new NERC standards for inverter-based resources, and expanded GADS reporting are increasing the work it takes to keep generation reliable and compliant. The 2027 Power Generation Reliability Summit brings NERC, generation executives and plant operators together to cover what 2027 requires and the AI tools generation owners are using to meet it.

Speakers announced in November

What you'll learn

From NERC's 2027 standards to AI at the plant

Hear what NERC requires of generation owners in 2027: PRC-029 and PRC-030 for inverter-based resources, Category 2 registration, EOP-012-3 cold weather requirements, and GADS reporting.

See how generation executives measure and fund reliability, what is driving forced outages, and where operators are using AI, automation and analytics at their plants today.
